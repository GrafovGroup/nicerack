# NiceRack.ca — Phase 2A Codex Master Brief: Catalogue Expansion & Organic Discovery

## Objective
Expand the existing national quote-only rack discovery site from 24 generic rack types to approximately 80–120 genuinely distinct rack types, improve useful SEO content, and strengthen product discovery. This is content/catalogue work, not new business software.

## Mandatory preflight
Read PHASE_1A_REPORT.md, PHASE_1B_REPORT.md, PHASE_1C_REPORT.md, PHASE_1D_REPORT.md, docs/CATALOGUE_EDITING.md, docs/ASSET_USAGE.md and the current catalogue/schema. Inspect current branch and recent commits. Preserve the live site's architecture, stable IDs, existing URLs, WHC configuration, phone and quote flows. If there are uncommitted changes, stop and report. Use a feature branch and PR. Do not deploy without authorization.

## Non-negotiable commercial rules
- All listings are REQUEST A QUOTE ONLY; no prices, ranges, Offer price schema, cart or checkout.
- CALL NOW uses (833) 696-9996 / tel:+18336969996.
- Quote requests go to info@nicerack.ca. No CRM, dashboard, login or lead database.
- Do not invent brands, suppliers, SKUs, capacities, dimensions, certifications, lead times, availability, warranties, reviews or installation coverage.
- New generic records must comply with existing example-type schema and be clearly labelled GENERAL RACK TYPE.
- No fake product photos, licensing claims or repeated placeholder images.

## Catalogue expansion
Aim for 10–15 useful generic rack types in each of eight divisions, with roughly 80–120 total after removing duplicates and near-synonyms. Prioritize:
1. Warehouse & Industrial: selective pallet, cantilever, drive-in, push-back, pallet-flow, carton-flow, boltless, longspan, pipe, tire, reel, sheet-metal, vertical and rack-protection systems.
2. Automotive & Fleet: van ladder, roof, truck-bed, headache, utility body, hitch bike, roof bike, cargo basket, kayak, ski/snowboard, trailer and fleet tool racks.
3. Garage & Home: overhead, wall rail, tool, bicycle, lumber, sports equipment, utility, pantry, laundry and shoe storage.
4. Retail & Display: garment, Z-rack, four-way clothing, gondola, gridwall, slatwall, display, shoe, literature, rolling and stockroom racks.
5. Fitness & Sports: power, squat, half rack, wall-folding, dumbbell, plate, kettlebell, medicine ball, yoga mat, ball and sports gear racks.
6. Restaurant & Hospitality: wire shelving, stainless shelving, dish, glass, bakery, sheet pan, drying, wine, luggage, housekeeping and commercial kitchen racks.
7. IT & Technology: floor server, wall network, open-frame, enclosed, audio-visual, telecom, battery and cable-management rack types.
8. Events & Rentals: coat, garment, portable display, backdrop support, folding, sign, crowd-management accessory, event storage and transport racks.

Choose only technically sensible distinct types. Avoid using 'rack' to describe unrelated products solely for SEO. Preserve all existing 24 slugs and valid deep links. Expand subcategory taxonomy only when useful. Add unique accurate descriptive copy: applications, what to consider before requesting a quote, practical fit/use cases and honest limitations. No unsupported engineering advice.

## SEO & UX
- Optimize existing eight division pages, new product-type pages, title/description/H1, breadcrumbs, internal links and sitemap; avoid keyword stuffing and near-duplicate pages.
- Prioritize customer intent and useful comparison/buying guidance. Add compact category FAQs only when substantive.
- Link related types naturally, improve discovery through search/filter and related-item modules without regressions.
- Keep prominent REQUEST A QUOTE and CALL NOW on mobile/desktop; preserve quote preselection and tracking context.
- Avoid autogenerated thin city pages, fake local addresses, location claims and fabricated social proof.
- Keep honest structured data: Organization/BreadcrumbList where valid; do not emit misleading Product/Offer schema for generic types.
- Images: use only approved, properly licensed distinct images with documented source/permission in docs/ASSET_USAGE.md. If not available, retain tasteful text-first cards and flag image acquisition as an external input; never scrape or fabricate supplier photography.

## Verification
Run catalogue validation, lint, typecheck, automated tests, production build, link/sitemap audit, and 320/390px mobile checks. Test quote prefill, comparison, search/filter, and phone links after expansion. Ensure route count, title uniqueness, and 404 handling. Add regression tests as needed.
