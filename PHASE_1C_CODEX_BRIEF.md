# NiceRack.ca — Simplified Phase 1C: Enquiry-Only Launch Readiness

## Business decision (supersedes previous Phase 1C scope)
NiceRack.ca is a straightforward Canadian rack-discovery and lead-generation site. Visitors discover products, submit an enquiry to info@nicerack.ca or call. No CRM, dashboard, staff accounts, lead database, quote-status pipeline, supplier portal, checkout, customer login or complex lead routing. Do not implement any of those.

## Preflight
Read PHASE_1A_REPORT.md, PHASE_1B_REPORT.md, README.md, docs/WHC_DEPLOYMENT.md and the current quote endpoint. Preserve Phase 1A and 1B code. Work on a feature branch and report changes. Do not redeploy without explicit approval.

## Work
1. Keep the existing quote/contact form simple and mobile-friendly: name, email, phone, company optional, city/province, selected rack type, quantity optional, message, delivery/installation needs optional, and privacy consent.
2. Ensure category/product preselection and source context remain intact.
3. Deliver validated enquiries directly to info@nicerack.ca using existing approved WHC transport. Keep origin checks, honeypot, validation, throttling and fail-closed behavior. Do not display success on failed delivery. Do not create persistent lead storage or CRM.
4. Provide clear customer-facing confirmation only when transport accepts the message; note inbox receipt requires separate manual verification. Avoid false claims about stock, lead times, suppliers, coverage or pricing.
5. Check CTA prominence on homepage, category pages, product pages, comparison and contact pages. Provide tap-to-call only with a confirmed working phone number; otherwise remove placeholder phone CTA.
6. Audit SEO basics, sitemap, metadata, category content, mobile UX and internal links. No thin autogenerated city pages.
7. Run lint, typecheck, tests, production build and form regression checks.
8. Update WHC deployment notes for DNS, HTTPS and real inbox-delivery verification; do not change DNS or send live test messages without approval.

## Deliverables
Commit focused changes and PHASE_1C_REPORT.md describing exactly what was changed, tests and remaining launch blockers. Do not implement features beyond this simplified enquiry-only scope.

## Definition of done
A visitor can browse the catalogue and submit a validated enquiry; when configured, the existing transport accepts it for delivery to info@nicerack.ca, and failure is clearly surfaced. No backend sales management features.
