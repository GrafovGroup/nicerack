# National coverage scope and editorial decisions

Reviewed October 16, 2026.

- [Government of Canada: provinces and territories](https://www.canada.ca/en/intergovernmental-affairs/services/provinces-territories.html) and [Canada’s regions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/discover-canada/read-online/canadas-regions.html) establish the ten-province, three-territory framework.
- [Statistics Canada reference maps](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/subjects/standard/sgc/2021/map-information) and [CMA/CA reference map](https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/geo/maps-cartes/referencemaps-cartesdereference/sgc-cgt/map-eng.cfm?SGC=01_B&wbdisable=true) provide the national urban-centre framework. The directory includes major metropolitan destinations, provincial and territorial capitals, regional centres and surrounding communities. It is a curated purchasing directory, not a population ranking or exhaustive municipal register.
- [Google Search spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) inform the decision to build useful bilingual province pages and a searchable destination directory instead of hundreds of thin near-identical standalone pages.

The 122 destination groups contain 397 named destinations. Regional groupings are browsing aids, not official CMA boundaries, service radii or statements that communities share a road connection. Geography alone does not establish installation availability, delivery timing, a branch office or inventory. Copy asks for the actual destination and project details and does not invent those claims.

Province pages have distinct practical receiving/handling considerations, a destination finder, quote links, and links to rack categories and guides. Existing city editorial pages retain their URLs and contextual content. Regional pages have reciprocal English/French canonical language relationships; query-based quote destinations are not additional sitemap pages.

## Imagery

Two new originals were generated with the built-in image-generation tool and visually reviewed. They show practical rack receiving and workshop environments; they are not represented as completed customer projects. Neutral alt text is customer-facing. Prompts and provenance are recorded internally in `docs/imagery/national-coverage-prompts.json` and the asset usage register.

The English and French directory each own a different image. Province, city and community links do not repeat either photograph. Existing product/service image placements remain unchanged. Exact SHA-256 and perceptual difference-hash comparison (distance <= 7) found no duplicate candidates across 1,032 accessible local raster assets. Inaccessible and remote-only prior-site inventories were not checked; no portfolio-wide verification is claimed.
