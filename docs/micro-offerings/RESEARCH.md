# Accessory opportunity research

Checked October 15, 2026. Manufacturer/distributor sources establish that these are useful accessory families, not NiceRack inventory, supplier relationships, universal compatibility, availability or performance guarantees. No third-party photographs were copied.

- Uline Canada [pallet racks and accessories](https://www.uline.ca/Grp_313/Pallet-Racks): decking, protection and shelf organization families.
- Uline Canada [rack protectors](https://www.uline.ca/BL_73/Rack-Protectors): exposed upright protection.
- Uline Canada [wire shelving casters](https://www.uline.ca/BL_4220/Wire-Shelving-Casters): model-specific replacement mobility components.
- Uline [slatwall accessories](https://www.uline.com/BL_380/Slatwall-Metal-Accessories?keywords=shelf): display hooks and fittings.
- StarTech [mounting screws and cage nuts](https://www.startech.com/en-us/server-management/rack-mounting-screws-cage-nuts): rack mounting hardware families.
- StarTech [blanking panels](https://www.startech.com/en-us/server-management/rack-blanking-panels?filter_uheight=4u): unused rack-space covers.
- StarTech [rack cable management](https://www.startech.com/en-us/server-management/server-rack-cable-management): rings, ducts and brush panel families.

The four small-service scopes are NiceRack enquiry options within existing planning and sourcing services. They do not claim engineering certification, inspection authority, electrical/network commissioning or guaranteed availability. Accessory relationships indicate relevant rack families, not verified fitment with every model.
