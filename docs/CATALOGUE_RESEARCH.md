## Distinctness review

- Preserve the 24 established broad URLs; add 64 focused types rather than creating synonyms for them. Broad descriptions explain when a more specific type is useful.
- No extra pages for a colour, finish, arbitrary capacity or size. No brands, fabricated SKUs, stock, warranties or delivery promises.
- Vehicle carriers describe transport; home bicycle racks describe parked storage. Retail clothing displays describe browsing; event coats describe cloakroom handling; backstage garment racks describe wardrobe operations.
- AV racks mount devices inside a frame; event road-case racks store loose cases. Event sign racks store dismantled panels, whereas display racks present items to visitors.
- Generic kitchen, industrial and weight shelving are retained as entry points; narrower pages describe different handling or fit requirements, not assumed technical upgrades.
- Specialist battery supports explicitly require qualified assessment. Event storage frames are not represented as live rigging or structural staging.
- Text similarity and unique-title checks supplement editorial review; they do not prove technical suitability. Product-specific facts require the separate evidence workflow before changing a generic record to verified.

## Phase 2B additions

24 further types distinguish support arrangement, stored item or retrieval method, not colour/size variants. A tire support is separate from carton shelving; a parts-bin interface differs from an open shelf; moving aisle banks differ from fixed longspan shelving. Van interior cargo and ladder retrieval methods are distinct from a base roof rack. Domestic board/rod/bag storage describes parked equipment. Retail roll/card/produce displays have different contact and browsing needs. Fitness additions store bars, bands and rollers rather than supporting a training exercise. Hospitality additions separate kegs, cutting boards and canned stock. Technology adds acoustic/environmental objectives and a hinged floor frame, without promising performance. Event additions store dismantled truss, cable coils and folded stands rather than acting as rigging.
